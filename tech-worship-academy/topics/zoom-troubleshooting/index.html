Lesson 1 · 15 min

When things go wrong

Recognize the most common hiccups and know the calm, simple fix for each.

ProblemFix
Someone's screen looks frozenAsk them in chat to leave and rejoin the meeting — this fixes the vast majority of freezes
People can see the slides but hear no sound from a videoWhen sharing your screen, make sure "share computer/system sound" was ticked before you clicked Share
A participant says they can't be heardCheck the Participants list — their microphone icon may show they're muted. Ask them to unmute, or unmute them yourself
Your own screen share looks wrong to othersConfirm with your AV partner what they're actually seeing, then Stop Share and re-share the correct window
The whole meeting freezes or crashes for you as hostHave a co-host or backup plan ready to start a fresh meeting immediately, so the service isn't left without a host
You don't need to fix everything instantly. A calm "one moment, we're sorting that out" in chat keeps people patient while you work through it.

Recording (only when asked)

Record the service correctly on the occasions it's requested — nothing more, nothing less.

  • Confirmed with your coordinator or pastor before service whether today's session should be recorded
  • If yes, clicked Record in the Zoom toolbar once the service is ready to begin
  • Confirmed the small recording indicator is showing — this confirms it's actually running
  • Used Pause Recording / Resume for any private moment not meant to be recorded
  • Clicked Stop Recording before ending the meeting
  • Checked with your coordinator where the finished file should go — recordings take a few minutes to finish saving
If you're not sure whether today needs a recording, ask rather than guess. It's easier to skip a recording than to recover one you forgot to start.

Lesson 3 · 10 min

Keeping the room safe

Protect the service from disruption without making it feel unwelcoming.

  • Once the regular latecomer window has passed (roughly 15–20 minutes in), consider locking the meeting from the Participants menu
  • If someone is disruptive, mute them immediately from the Participants list
  • If muting isn't enough, open Participants, hover over their name, and choose Remove
  • Let your coordinator know afterward if anything unusual happened, so it can be reviewed
These tools exist for rare situations — most services never need them, but it's good to know where they are.

Lesson 4 · 10 min

Ending properly

Close the service in a way that actually ends the meeting for everyone.

  • Confirmed any recording has been stopped
  • Clicked the red End button in the bottom-right corner of Zoom
  • Chose End Meeting for All — this closes the meeting completely for everyone still in it
  • Avoided clicking Leave Meeting by itself, since that only removes you and can leave the meeting open with no host
  • Checked with your AV partner that everything wrapped up cleanly on their side too
Service just ended and you need to leave. What should you click?
"Leave Meeting" only removes you and can leave the meeting running with no host. As host, you almost always want "End Meeting for All" so the meeting actually closes for everyone.
