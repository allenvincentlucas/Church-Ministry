# 30-Day Guitar Journey for Worship Team Musicians — Content Calendar
Matches: tech-worship-academy/topics/guitar-beginner-30day/index.html (MU.04, Music Ministry)

Built for church and worship team guitarists. Every week pairs the technique focus with real worship songs to play along with — most sourced directly from the Worship Chord Library, so what you practice is repertoire you can actually bring to the team.

---

## Week 1 — Foundations & Open Chords
| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Guitar anatomy: parts (headstock, nut, frets, bridge) and how they affect sound |
| 2 | Holding the guitar & pick correctly, tuning by ear vs. tuner |
| 3 | Learn G, C, D — the three chords that unlock hundreds of songs |
| 4 | Fix the #1 beginner mistake: pressing far harder than the string needs |
| 5 | Add the rest of the open chord set: A, E, Am, Em, Dm |
| 6 | Chord-switching drill: alternate G↔C cleanly for one full minute |
| 7 | Rest & review: replay every chord from memory, no chart |

**Technique videos:** *8 Open Chords That You Should Know!*, *The 8 Essential Beginner Chord Grips* (JustinGuitar)

**Play along:**
- **Let Him Enter** — G, D, C, D7 — [Worship Chord Library](../../../worship-chord-library/site/songs/let-him-enter.html)
- **Goodness Of God** — Capo 1

## Week 4 — Theory, Made Simple + 30-Day Review
| Day | Focus |
|---|---|

### Notes
- Days are grouped into 4 lesson modules on the live page (matching your other topic pages' rail-nav format), not 30 separate lesson entries.
- Song keys, capos, and YouTube links for Let Him Enter, I Can Trust You, All Creation Sing, and No Greater Love are pulled directly from their `.cho` files in `worship-chord-library/songs/` — not from generic internet chord sites — so they match what's already live on your site.
- Let Him Enter has no video in the chord library (checked the `.cho` and the built page) — it's linked as a chord-chart-only reference, not embedded as a video, to avoid a broken or invented link.
- Every JustinGuitar technique video is a real, verified link — no placeholder/invented IDs.
- Trust In You and Unang Alay from the chord library were considered but left out of this course: both use chord shapes (Eb/Bb/Gm, and jazz-leaning maj7/sus chords) that are more advanced than an intermediate-level roadmap should require — better suited to a future, more advanced module.
